Waterfront park opening moves to spring

#Waterfront#CityNews#Parks

The new waterfront park that was due to open this autumn will now open in spring, the city said on Tuesday.

Officials said the paths and lighting along the shore need more time to finish, and that they would rather open late than open a park that is not ready.

The old pier next to the site stays open in the meantime, and the weekend market will keep its usual place until the park is finished.
